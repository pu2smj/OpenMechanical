OpenBearingWorkbench Manifesto

Engineering should never be limited by the absence of standard components.

We believe engineering knowledge should be:

Open
Reproducible
Verifiable
Parametric
Reusable

We believe engineers should spend their time creating new solutions—not recreating existing standards.

Every standardized component contributed to this project is one less repetitive task for future engineers.

Design the innovation.
Let the library handle the standards.
